# Chief of Staff (starter)

A small Claude plugin that sets up a personal AI chief of staff in a folder you own. Run it in Cowork inside a project. The setup interview reads your recent sent email and calendar (if you connect them), drafts a picture of you for you to correct, then writes your standing instructions, task list and memory as plain Markdown files you can open and edit.

Commands: `/chief-of-staff:setup`, `/chief-of-staff:start-my-day`, `/chief-of-staff:wrap-up`. Or just say "set me up", "start my day", "wrap up" and "remember that…".

Nothing is sent, accepted, paid or deleted without your explicit yes to the exact text.

Install: Customize, Plugins, Add marketplace, then paste this repository. Connect Gmail and Google Calendar under Customize, Connectors.
